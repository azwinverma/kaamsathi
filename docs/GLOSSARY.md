# KaamSathi — Glossary (UI terms)

Use the term people in Nepal's workplaces actually say. In English UI, keep the
Nepali word in the label where it is the common term (e.g. "Hajiri (Attendance)").
In Nepali UI use the Devanagari column. Keys are used by web (`messages/*.json`)
and mobile (`lib/l10n/*.arb`) — keep identical keys.

## BS months (index 1–12)
Baishakh बैशाख · Jestha जेठ · Asar असार · Shrawan साउन · Bhadra भदौ · Ashwin असोज ·
Kartik कात्तिक · Mangsir मंसिर · Poush पुस · Magh माघ · Falgun फागुन · Chaitra चैत

Nepal's fiscal year runs Shrawan 1 → Asar end (e.g. FY 2083/84 = Shrawan 2083 – Asar 2084).
